# Jury Executive Brief: Commercial Viability and Path to Pilot
**PREVIA Health AI — Future Health Digital Innovation Challenge 2026**
*Executive Brief for the Challenge Evaluation Jury (Reading time: 2 minutes)*

---

### 1. Who needs PREVIA?
**Public health agencies, global health NGOs, and primary care networks** conducting cardiovascular screening campaigns under severe budget and capacity constraints. They cannot screen entire populations at once and must objectively prioritize who to evaluate first without wasting limited clinical hours or abandoning remote peripheral communities.

---

### 2. Why this solution?
Because PREVIA does not offer an opaque black-box algorithm or an isolated web calculator, but an **integrated decision-support and allocation optimization platform**:
- **Zero Laboratory Dependency**: Certified deterministic implementation of the 2019 non-lab WHO CVD risk chart using 5 non-invasive clinical parameters (blood pressure, age, smoking, BMI).
- **Capacity-Constrained Optimization**: Models and enforces strict screening quotas (15%, 25%, 50%), doubling the capture of priority high-risk individuals compared to opportunistic walk-in screening (proven across 360 runs in Chantier 15).
- **Documented Territorial Equity**: Restricts inter-sector selection rate disparities below 2 percentage points.
- **Auditable Transparency**: Integrated SHAP feature explainability and a sovereign, hallucination-free RAG assistant grounded strictly in official WHO/CDC sources.

---

### 3. Who would fund it?
- **Near-term (Pilot Phase)**: Future Health Challenge prize capital combined with innovation seed grants from international health NGOs (e.g., Doctors of the World, ALIMA, Red Cross).
- **Medium to long-term (Commercial Scale)**: Multilateral development funders (USAID, The Global Fund, World Bank, bilateral agencies), national health ministry NCD programs, and corporate occupational health mutuals.

---

### 4. How can it be deployed?
Through a lean, friction-free hybrid deployment model:
- **Technically**: Ultra-lightweight containerized Docker architecture, deployable in sovereign cloud environments (observed baseline: ~€62/month across Netcup/Vercel) or in fully offline progressive web app (PWA) mode on field laptops in zero-connectivity zones.
- **Commercially**: Turnkey outreach campaign packages (€2,500 for 2,500 profiles) and multi-site institutional annual licenses (€15,000/year for healthcare networks).

---

### 5. What would the initial pilot look like?
A **strictly supervised 6-month operational pilot with a partner health NGO** evaluating 500 to 1,000 eligible adult participants (aged 40–74):
- *4-Phase Protocol*: Ethical clearance & local parameterization (M1) ➔ Clinician training & 50-profile dry run (M2) ➔ Supervised field screening with 100% clinician oversight (M3–M4) ➔ Independent statistical audit and joint reporting (M5–M6).
- *Pseudonymized Data*: Zero persistence of nominative personal identifiers, complete privacy compliance.
- *Strict Safety Gates*: Immediate pilot suspension in the event of any algorithmic deviation or unsupervised use.

---

### 6. What measurable outcomes would justify investment?
Successful validation across our **12-KPI evaluation matrix**, including:
1. **Record completeness ≥ 98%** and mean screening duration **≤ 90 seconds** per intake.
2. **Deterministic WHO concordance of 100%** with zero mathematical calculation discrepancies.
3. **Inter-sector selection gap ≤ 5 percentage points** (proven allocative equity).
4. **Direct marginal infrastructure cost < €0.50 per evaluated profile**.
5. **High clinician usability** (System Usability Scale score ≥ 80 / 100).
6. **Prevention pathway 30-day confirmation rate ≥ 60%** for priority high-risk profiles.

---

### 7. Why support this project beyond the hackathon?
1. **Exceptional Engineering Maturity**: This is not a concept or pitch deck. The platform is fully bilingual, deployed in production, and hardened by over 140 automated non-regression tests.
2. **Proven Financial Viability**: Minimal fixed overhead (< €4,000/year), achieving operational break-even on just **4 campaigns or a single institutional annual license**.
3. **Massive and Measurable Real-World Impact**: A production-ready solution capable of immediately transforming cardiovascular prevention efficiency wherever clinical laboratories are absent.
